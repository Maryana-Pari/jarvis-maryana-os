How JARVIS prioritizes

The current prototype uses a deterministic scoring rule, not a language model. Urgency increases as a deadline approaches. Impact raises priority, while larger tasks receive a small effort penalty. Every score is a heuristic, not a guarantee of the objectively best decision.
